# Hole World – Ultimate Multiplayer · Game Brief

**Pitch (the person's words, kept):** "Real-time 1v1 multiplayer. Players control a black hole that swallows
objects. Bigger holes can swallow smaller opponents. When a player dies, they respawn at level 1. The surviving
player keeps their current size and progress." Three worlds: Barbie-style dream world, Baby Bunny World,
Berlin Meme City.

**Inspired by** the "swallow-everything hole" genre — original mechanics, worlds and assets (Avoid: any
existing game's names, art, logos, levels). The pink world is named *Pink Dream World* (no toy-brand names);
the Berlin designer caps use an invented monogram, not a real brand.

## First minute
1. Pick a name, avatar and hole colour; choose a world (or Random).
2. Quick Match (or a private code). The lobby waits — the match starts **only** with two players.
3. 3-2-1-GO: you are a tiny hole on a road intersection. Pfand bottles, carrots and lipsticks fit; cars don't yet.
4. Each swallow grows you; chained swallows build a combo multiplier; level-ups offer an upgrade card.
5. A *"You can swallow your rival!"* banner appears once you are 12 % bigger — hunt them down.

## Challenge
Racing your rival for the best feeding grounds vs. the risk of being eaten (respawn at level 1 = losing all
size). Power-ups and the vortex ability create swing moments; the last 30 s speed up the music.

## Features (built)
Matchmaking + private rooms · reconnect · 3 worlds (~800 objects each, ~90 drawn models) · 5 power-ups ·
dash + vortex · 3 upgrade tracks · combos · kill feed · leaderboard · EN/DE · touch, mouse, keyboard, gamepad.
